How to read this table

All: exit cost with every source the aggregator routes through. No RFQ: the same sale with off-chain RFQ quotes excluded (KyberSwap's excludeRFQSources) - on-chain pools only, including pools run by market makers. Dashed ring: under the break-even with RFQ, at or above it or no route without - such markets come first. Underlined, on sand: the sale costs at least the break-even. RFQ share: the part of the collateral sold to RFQ quotes. Markets with no route at any size are only on the exit depth page. Every term: glossary.
