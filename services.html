Exit-risk review of your markets

For vault curators, risk teams and protocols with Morpho Blue markets on Base. You get the public measurements redone at the sizes of your own positions, with a price-move scenario, as a private report. The first review is free.

What you get

  • A report of 4-6 pages (PDF) and the numbers as a CSV.
  • Clearance of each of your markets at the sizes of your largest positions and at sizes you choose, not only at $25k, $200k and $1M.
  • Quote dependence: how much of the exit rests on RFQ (request for quote) market makers, and what on-chain pools alone give.
  • Collateral near liquidation by position size: how much sits in positions one liquidation can clear and how much has to be liquidated in parts.
  • What a 10% and a 20% price move in the collateral do to the exit cost and to the positions close to liquidation.
  • Change since the first snapshot (13 Nov 2026), once there is a history to show.
  • A line in the report for each number: how it was measured, so your team can repeat it.

How it works

  1. Send your vault name or market ids on Telegram or by email (the buttons below open a message). Position sizes or limits you want used are optional.
  2. You get a reply within 2 days.
  3. You get the review within 5 business days.

Terms

  • The first review is free, with no obligation. After that: a fixed fee or a regular note, agreed in writing before any work.
  • Private: data you send us is not used by our bots and is not published. The review stays with you and never changes the public pages.
  • Findings and numbers, not recommendations: what to do with them is your decision. Not investment advice.
  • Conflict of interest: the operator also runs liquidation bots on Base. See About & disclosure.
