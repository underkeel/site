Morpho Blue on Base · exit depth · snapshot 2026-11-15 18:23 UTC

21 of 47 Morpho markets on Base clear a $1M liquidation; 18 break at some size, 2 clear only with RFQ quotes, 5 have no route and 1 was not fully measured

Underkeel measures whether each Morpho Blue market on Base can still be liquidated at size. Every 6 hours it prices selling the seized collateral at $25k, $200k and $1M and compares the cost with the liquidation bonus, in all 47 markets with at least $100k borrowed.

The 21 markets that clear every size hold $1.65B, 77% of the $2.15B borrowed. The 18 markets that break hold $358M; the largest is USDe/USDC 91.5% with $340M borrowed: a $200k liquidation clears, selling $1M of USDe costs 37.7% against a 2.6% bonus. Positions that large are liquidated in parts across blocks; the site measures one liquidation in one transaction. 5 markets with $94.0M have no DEX route and are shown separately.

The bonus

A higher LLTV leaves less room

Morpho fixes the bonus by the market's LLTV:

bonus = min(15%, 1/(0.3 x LLTV + 0.7) - 1)

A 62.5% LLTV pays 12.7%; a 94.5% LLTV pays 1.7%. The exit cost has to stay below that for a single liquidation to pay. Definitions.
