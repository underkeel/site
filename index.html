Morpho Blue on Base - exit depth

77% of the $2.14B borrowed on Morpho Base sits in markets that clear a $1M liquidation

Underkeel measures whether each Morpho Blue market on Base can still be liquidated at size. Every 6 hours it prices selling the seized collateral at $25k, $200k and $1M and sets the cost against what the liquidation bonus leaves room for, in all 47 markets with at least $100k borrowed.

18 of 47 markets clear every measured size; 12 break at some size, 8 break only at a sale larger than their whole debt, 1 clears only with RFQ quotes, 5 have no route and 3 were not fully measured. The 12 markets that break hold $390M; the largest is USDe/USDC 91.5% with $326M borrowed: a $200k liquidation clears, selling $1M of USDe costs 37.7% against a break-even of 2.55%. Positions that large are liquidated in parts across blocks; the site measures one liquidation in one transaction.

The keel - bonus and break-even by LLTV

A higher LLTV leaves less water to begin with

Morpho fixes the bonus by the market's LLTV, and the bonus fixes the exit cost at which a liquidation breaks even:

bonus = min(15%, 1/(0.3 x LLTV + 0.7) - 1)
break-even exit cost = 1 - 1/(1 + bonus)

A 62.5% LLTV pays 12.7% and breaks even at 11.25%; a 94.5% LLTV pays 1.7%. The exit has to cost less than the break-even for a single liquidation to pay. Definitions.

Upright figures are fixed by the protocol or reported by Morpho; italic figures are this site's own soundings and change with every snapshot.
