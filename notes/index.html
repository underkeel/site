  1. 21 Nov 2026 · What changed · snapshot 2026-11-20 06:22 UTC

    USDe/USDC debt fell $94.7M in ten hours; its $1M exit held at 38%

    From 14 to 20 November, USDe/USDC borrowers took 90.3M USDe out with their repayments and sold none of it in those transactions, SOL/USDC 62.5% gained pool depth, and cbLTC/USDC 62.5% lost a maker.

  2. 19 Nov 2026 · Event note · snapshot 2026-11-18 06:23 UTC

    Borrowers made 60% of the debt in reach safe before the November low

    Between 12 and 15 November, the Morpho markets on Base lending USDC at the lowest LLTV, 62.5%, had 9.0% of their debt liquidated. Those at 86% had 5.2%.

  3. 18 Nov 2026 · Method note · snapshot 2026-11-17 06:23 UTC

    A 12.68% bonus breaks even at an 11.25% exit cost

    Until 16 November this site set the cost of selling seized collateral against the liquidation bonus. A liquidator breaks even at a lower cost: 11.25% when the bonus is 12.68%. This note is the correction.

  4. 17 Nov 2026 · Market note · snapshot 2026-11-17 06:23 UTC

    A $1M USDe sale on Base costs 37.7% against a 2.55% break-even

    USDe/USDC 91.5% is the second largest Morpho market on Base, with $329M borrowed. On 17 November a $200k sale of USDe cost 0.10%, and a $1M sale cost 37.7%.

  5. 17 Nov 2026 · Event note · snapshot 2026-11-17 06:23 UTC

    Two minutes when a 12.7% bonus was not enough

    On 8 November cbXRP traded 8-9% below Coinbase on Base. In the cbXRP/USDC 62.5% market on Morpho, 140 liquidations waited between 4.6 and 80 seconds for a taker.
