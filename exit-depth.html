How to read this table

Status: the first size where a single liquidation stops paying. Clears to: the clearing size, the largest measured size with positive clearance; "+": positive at the largest measured size, larger sizes are not measured. A single liquidation larger than that has to be split across blocks (measured up to $1M). Bonus: min(15%, 1/(0.3 x LLTV + 0.7) - 1), fixed by the market's LLTV (liquidation loan-to-value). Exit: share lost selling that much collateral into the loan token, best KyberSwap route including RFQ (request for quote) market makers, both sides valued by KyberSwap; "clearance" in the switch above the table shows the bonus minus the exit cost, in percentage points (pp), instead. Costs above 50% are shown as >50%: there no liquidity and a wrong price look the same. The exit is measured once per collateral/loan pair, so markets of one pair share it and differ in the bonus. Collateral, HF < 1.1 / 1.25: collateral of positions within 10% and 20% of liquidation by health factor (the second includes the first). peg: a pegged pair, where the health factor tracks the peg, not the market. 0x... after a market: the start of its id, where two markets share a pair and an LLTV.
