Summary

What this means for a curator of cbXRP/USDC 62.5%

Position by position this market clears today. The risk is hundreds of small borrowers that cross the liquidation threshold together: from a 12% fall in cbXRP on, more collateral comes up for liquidation than one liquidation clears.

  1. One liquidation clears about $840k of cbXRP with all sources and about $560k on-chain only: the largest sale whose exit cost stays under the 11.25% break-even, the cost at which the liquidation bonus no longer pays for the sale. Both come from the first of 3 rounds of quotes, when a $1M sale cost 16.9%; in the third it cost 11.26%, so the limit moves with the quotes. The $500k sale clears (2.0%); the $2M and $5M sales cost more than half their value in one sale, so they pay only in parts, if the pools refill between them - not measured here.
  2. The difference is RFQ market makers - firms that sign a price off chain for each sale - and here mostly one of them: it took 72-84% of the volume of every sale from $25k to $500k. Without RFQ makers one liquidation clears a third less, $560k. The quotes move: in the site's 35 snapshots since 2026-11-14, a $1M sale was at a loss in 27 with all sources and in all 35 on-chain only.
  3. Position size is not the issue in this market today. 1,900 positions are within 20% of liquidation - a 20% fall in cbXRP would make them liquidatable - and a full liquidation of any of them sells less than $250k, under what one liquidation clears on-chain only.
  4. Between an 11% and a 13% fall in cbXRP the liquidatable positions jump from 71 to 401, and their sale from $486k to $2.4M. From a 12% fall on, the sale is more than one liquidation clears, with all sources or on-chain only. A 20% fall makes the same 1,900 positions liquidatable. Each clears on its own; together they are $11.1M to sell, 13 times what one liquidation clears with all sources; selling it in one sale at today's depth would cost more than half its value.
What does not follow

Not a forecast of bad debt. Morpho Blue liquidations can be partial and follow one another, and the pools refill as arbitrage brings cbXRP on Base back to its price elsewhere, at a pace not measured here. After both falls no position holds less collateral than its debt.

Not a judgement of the market, its collateral or any vault, and not a recommendation. Makers may quote more in a fall, or stop quoting.

The depth is today's, and a fall can thin it: on 2026-11-08, in the two minutes when the cbXRP pools on Base traded 5-11% below the market's oracle, a $25k sale through them cost 37-40% (event note).

6 - method

How each number was measured

  • Market: Morpho Blue on Base, cbXRP/USDC 62.5%, id 0xd4a903dc; $47.7M borrowed of $57.8M supplied; 10,923 borrowing positions.
  • Bonus: min(15%, 1/(0.3 x LLTV + 0.7)) - 1 = 12.68%. Break-even exit cost: 1 - 1/(1 + bonus) = 11.25%.
  • Exit cost: KyberSwap Aggregator's public API, the best route selling the collateral into USDC on Base at each size; cost = 1 - value out / value in, both as KyberSwap prices them. On-chain only: the same request with RFQ sources excluded. 3 rounds 60 s apart; the tables show the first, section 2 all of them.
  • One liquidation clears: the largest sale under the break-even, searched in the first round by halving, in log steps, the gap between the largest measured size that clears and the smallest that does not (7 quotes per route); the lower end of the last bracket, rounded down to two significant figures.
  • Positions: Morpho API, every position with a borrow and a health factor below 1.5: health factor, collateral and debt in USD. Nothing else of a position is read; they leave the review only as counts and sums, and a group of fewer than 3 positions without its sums.
  • Sale of a full liquidation: min(debt x (1 + bonus), collateral after the fall). A fall: health factor x (1 - fall), with the USDC debt held at $1.
  • Record: the market's row in data/exit-depth.json of every snapshot in the site's public history (35 snapshots).
  • Not measured: the pace at which the pools refill after a sale, how makers quote in a fall, the oracle's path, gas.
  • Measured 2026-11-23 16:48:42 UTC to 16:52 UTC: 54 KyberSwap quotes. Method: Clearance v3, as on the method page.

Every number is in the CSV with its unit, so a team can load it and check every figure.
