About

About & disclosure

Who runs this site, where the numbers come from, how it is paid for and how errors are corrected.

Who runs it

Underkeel is run by Dmitrii Luzin, an independent developer. I also run liquidation bots on Base, so I may profit from liquidations in the markets measured here. That is a conflict of interest, and I would rather you read it here than find it. You do not have to trust me: every number is repeatable from public data (Method, Data).

What the site does not publish

Borrower addresses, my own positions, bots or strategies. It publishes market-wide measurements only.

Money

Underkeel has no token and no sponsor. Its costs are a domain, free static hosting and the operator's time. If it receives a grant or fees from a protocol, a curator or any market participant, the source, the amount and the date will be listed here.

Paid work

One service: an exit-risk review of a curator's markets (Services). A review is private to the client and never changes the public numbers; a client cannot buy a different measurement of its market. Clients are named here only with their consent.

Why "Underkeel"

Under-keel clearance is the water left between a ship's keel and the seabed. Underkeel measures the same margin for a lending market: what the liquidation bonus leaves room for, minus the cost of selling the collateral.

Visit counts

The site counts page views, how long a page stays visible, how far it is scrolled and which links are followed out, to the data files or to the contacts. With each view it keeps the page that linked here (site and path, no query), the country and the kind of device (desktop, phone, tablet). It sets no cookies and keeps no IP address or browser string: a visitor is a hash of the address and the browser mixed with a random key that is replaced every day, and the counts never join one day's visitors to another's. Events are deleted after 60 days. The counter is a small script sending to the site's own collector on Cloudflare; with scripts off nothing is sent. Only the operator sees the counts.

Data sources and terms

  • Exit quotes: the KyberSwap Aggregator public API, best route on Base for each size, with and without RFQ quotes.
  • Markets, prices and positions: the Morpho API.
  • Liquidations: Morpho Blue events on Base, read from the chain.

Each source's terms apply to what is derived from it. The numbers are measurements of public data at the snapshot time; how each one is made is on the Method page, the files on Data.

Independence, not investment advice

Not affiliated with Morpho, Base, KyberSwap or Chainlink. Informational only. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to supply, borrow, liquidate or change a market's parameters. Exit costs are a snapshot: in a fast market they rise exactly when liquidations are needed.

Corrections

  • 16 Nov 2026: Until this date every clearance on the site was the liquidation bonus minus the exit cost. A liquidation breaks even earlier, at an exit cost of 1 - 1/(1 + bonus), so the published clearances were too high by 0.01 to 1.43 percentage points (the most at LLTV 62.5%: bonus 12.68%, break-even 11.25%). Corrected in Clearance v3; statuses on the snapshot of 16 Nov 12:23 UTC did not change.

Every correction is listed here with its date: what was shown, what is right, which snapshots and pages it touched, and when it was fixed. Published JSON files are not rewritten; the history stays in git.
