How to read this chart

Higher means a costlier exit. Seabed: exit cost on the best route, RFQ makers included; dashed - on-chain pools only. Keel: the break-even exit cost; a liquidation pays while the seabed stays under it. Arrows: the clearance at each size; darker blue - under 2 pp left; sand above the keel - a loss. Three sizes are measured; the lines between them are a guide.
